[color=#00f0ff][size=5][b]0. WHAT THIS IS[/b][/size][/color]
The District Guide brings the NC Zoning Board map into the game. It knows which district you are standing in, and tells you what the Board has on file for it.

Everything you see comes from the NC Zoning Board registry at nczoning.net, fetched by NC Zoning Board - Core. This mod displays it. If a location mod is missing here, it is missing from the registry - and the map is open for submissions.
[line]
[color=#00f0ff][size=5][b]1. THE FOUR SURFACES[/b][/size][/color]
[*][b]District notice.[/b] Cross into a new district and a panel appears under the game's own banner: how many mapped locations are here, and the nearest by name.
[*][b]World map.[/b] Hover any district and its info panel gains a count and a category breakdown, in the map's own type and colors.
[*][b]The guide.[/b] One key opens a full browser - every district, every location, with images and search.
[*][b]Fast travel.[/b] Arriving fires no district banner in vanilla, so the notice shows up on its own.

Each can be switched off on its own. With all four off, the game behaves exactly as if the mod were not installed.
[line]
[color=#00f0ff][size=5][b]2. USING THE GUIDE[/b][/size][/color]
[*][b]Open it[/b] with the key set under District Guide. The default is the apostrophe.
[*][b]Browse[/b] by district and subdistrict. It opens on wherever you are standing.
[*][b]Search[/b] by name, author or tag.
[*][b]SHOW ON MAP[/b] drops a waypoint on the location, opens the world map on it and starts routing. The same button reads CLEAR WAYPOINT once one is set.
[*][b]TELEPORT[/b] takes you there. Not available while you are in a vehicle - get out first.
[*][b]Click a card's image[/b] for the full-size photograph. Click anywhere to close it.
[*][b]Green "RECENTLY UPDATED"[/b] on a card image marks locations changed lately. They sort to the front.
[*][b]Cyan "INSTALLED"[/b] on a card image marks the ones you already have. It appears while SHOWING: ALL, and needs Cyber Engine Tweaks.
[line]
[color=#00f0ff][size=5][b]3. SETTINGS[/b][/size][/color]
All of them are in this hub, under NC Zoning District Guide.

[b]Locations[/b]
[*][b]Narrow to Subdistrict.[/b] Scopes every count to your subdistrict when you are in one, rather than the whole district. Applies to all four surfaces.

[b]District Guide[/b]
[*][b]Enable District Guide.[/b] Turns the browser on or off.
[*][b]Open Guide Key.[/b] The key that opens it. Needs Input Loader.
[*][b]Open Guide Modifier.[/b] An optional key to hold alongside it. Any key, not just Shift, Alt or Ctrl. Leave unset for none.
[*][b]Open Guide Showing.[/b] Which locations the guide lists when it opens. You can still switch inside the guide. Needs Cyber Engine Tweaks.
[*][b]Open on Your District.[/b] The guide opens on the district you are standing in. Off opens it on ALL LOCATIONS, as does being off the map.
[*][b]Open the Map on Show.[/b] SHOW ON MAP opens the world map and centers it on the waypoint. Off places the waypoint and leaves you in the guide.
[*][b]Track the Waypoint.[/b] Routes to the waypoint straight away. This replaces a custom waypoint you have placed; your tracked quest is a separate slot and is left alone.

[b]World Map[/b]
[*][b]Show on World Map.[/b] Adds the count and category breakdown to the map's district info panel.

[b]District Notice[/b]
[*][b]Enable District Notice.[/b] The panel under the game's district banner.
[*][b]Name the Nearest Location.[/b] Also names the closest location in the area. Off shows only the count.
[*][b]Show on Fast Travel.[/b] The arrival panel after a fast travel. Off leaves fast travel entirely to the game.
[line]
[color=#00f0ff][size=5][b]4. THE INSTALL FILTER[/b][/size][/color]
With Cyber Engine Tweaks installed, the guide marks which of these location mods you already have, and one button cycles the list:
[*][b]ALL[/b] - every location on file.
[*][b]INSTALLED[/b] - only the ones already on your machine.
[*][b]MISSING[/b] - only the ones you do not have.
[*][b]UNKNOWN[/b] - the ones that cannot be detected either way. AMM location mods are the usual case: they ship a single file into CET's own folder, which no mod can read.

[color=#ffb300]Without Cyber Engine Tweaks the filter button is hidden.[/color] Everything else is unchanged.
[line]
[color=#00f0ff][size=5][b]5. A KNOWN LIMIT OF THE ROAD NETWORK[/b][/size][/color]
A waypoint only starts routing once the world map has been opened. The guide does that for you, so you will not normally meet this.

You will meet it if you turn both [b]Open the Map on Show[/b] and [b]Track the Waypoint[/b] off. The pin is then placed but draws no route until you open the map and track it yourself.
[line]
[color=#00f0ff][size=5][b]6. IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG[/b][/size][/color]
[*][b]No counts anywhere, or "NO LOCATION DATA".[/b] The registry did not load. That is the Core's side - check its requirements, and whether you have RedHttpClient or a hand-placed data file.
[*][b]A district reads zero.[/b] Some have none on file; NCX Spaceport / Morro Rock is one. Zero and "no data" are different answers, and the guide says which.
[*][b]Cards show a placeholder instead of a photograph.[/b] RedIMGRetriever is missing, or that location has no image on file.
[*][b]The open key does nothing.[/b] Check it under District Guide, and that Input Loader is installed.
[*][b]Anything else.[/b] The log is at r6\logs\mods\NCZoningDistrictGuide__<date_time>.log, one file per session. It opens with a [CFG] line listing every setting and a [READY] line saying how much data arrived. You can read it in this hub under LOGS.
